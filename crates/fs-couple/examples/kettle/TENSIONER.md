# Force-driven edge tensioning during a strike

The optional edge actuator is a finite-inertia mechanical coordinate, not a
frequency control. It changes actual membrane prestress and receives the head's
reciprocal reaction. The original nonlinear stretching, felt mallet histories,
interior pressure and selected exterior load stay in the same prepared solve.

```sh
# Illustrative effective hardware, NOT measured timpani pedal parameters.
# M=0.8 kg, extra K=4000 N/m, drag=20 Ns/m, |edge strain| <= 0.002.
# At 768 kHz, apply 80 N at 0.05 s and release at 0.1 s.
cargo run --release -p fs-couple --example kettle -- \
  crates/fs-couple/examples/kettle/illustrative-parabolic.fsk 115200 \
  --edge-tensioner 0.8 4000 20 0.002 \
  --edge-force 38400 80 --edge-force 76800 0 > tensioned-motion.csv

(set -C; cargo run --release -p fs-couple --example kettle -- \
  crates/fs-couple/examples/kettle/illustrative-parabolic.fsk 7200 \
  --wav 20 40 500 32 12 0.4 0.3 1.2 \
  --edge-tensioner 0.8 4000 20 0.002 \
  --edge-force 38400 80 --edge-force 76800 0 > tensioned-kettle.wav)
```

`--edge-force SAMPLE FORCE_N` addresses a **mechanical** sample, even for WAV.
Assignments must be strictly increasing, lie before the requested final sample,
and fit the force budget. Initially force is zero; each assignment is held until
the next, including nonzero final assignments. Positive force acts radially
outward. Nothing resets contact, vibration, material memory or pitch at an event.
A rejected tick consumes no event because selection uses only accepted time.
No edge options preserves the original fixed-rim model and CSV columns.

## Actual constitutive coupling

`fs_plate::shell::head::dilation::DilationReduction` reuses the original relaxed
von Karman reduction. For radial displacement s and clear radius R, it adds
uniform strain s/R. The homogeneous isotropic film contributes

```
Delta T = E*h/(1-nu) * s/R
G_ij = integral grad(phi_i).grad(phi_j) dA
H = H_original + Delta T * q^T G q / 2 + (K_film + K_extra)*s^2/2
K_film = 2*E*h/(1-nu) * actual_polygon_area/R^2
```

These cross terms equal those from the original positive facet strain energy:
the relaxed in-plane field vanishes on the rim, so the area integral of its
divergence is zero. No Berger-only replacement, hand-authored partial list or
second in-plane solve is introduced. The full mixed Hessian is retained. Hot
coupling adds O(modes^2) work without allocations or repeated eigenanalysis;
that operation count is not an end-to-end real-time benchmark.

The final body coordinate is sqrt(M)*s. Physical force maps to canonical effort
by 1/sqrt(M), so supply is force times radial displacement. M is the **total**
effective inertia represented by that coordinate, including any hardware/film
inertia already reduced into it. Extra stiffness is in parallel with computed
film stiffness; damping enters as drag/M. Do not count those components twice.
Static installed preload is balanced at s=0. Reported work is incremental about
that equilibrium, not total foot work against an identified commercial linkage.

The shared impact host enforces positive baseline tension, the supplied edge
strain cap and the head's existing transverse slope cap at initialization and
before every accepted endpoint. Failed acceptance preserves all state/history.
CSV adds outward edge displacement, uniform baseline tension and complete step
supply. Baseline tension excludes spatial stress generated by head vibration.

## Deliberate model limits

This is a uniform small-strain edge-actuation image, **not a complete production
pedal/hoop/bearing-edge mechanism**. It does not identify leverage, contact around
the rim, skin sliding, hoop flexibility or spatially unequal lug tension. The
fixed-reference head/cavity/acoustic geometry and quasi-static internal in-plane
relaxation are unchanged. Radial edge motion is not a new normal sound source;
only the original head modes drive exterior air. Tuning can move frequencies and
nonlinear harmonics beyond the acoustic band: the existing sampled fits are not
full-band or continuously converged sound certificates. The retained transverse
basis needs its own adequacy check over the desired tuning range.

The role of tension in drum/timpani sound is established, but these particular
hardware values are illustrations, not a fit to measurements. See the primary
study *Sound model of an orchestral kettledrum considering viscoelastic effects*
(https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0003682X19311211). This
implementation derives its coupling from the existing film energy instead of
adopting that paper's complete constitutive or acoustic model.

## Checks

Six fs-plate regressions cover the physical prestress increment, film stiffness,
fixed-rim limit, normalization and exact mixed derivatives. Five impact-host
regressions cover force work, head reaction, tangent paths and refused states.
Three example regressions cover sample controls and actual felt/head/air/load
composition. They have been authored but not run locally: Cargo/rustc are absent.
The existing percussion CI selectors include the new module and example tests.

Independent Python FEM/equation checks ran on 49 nodes, 80 facets and three
transverse modes. Across 30 states, the cross-matrix energy and positive-facet
energy agreed within 3.20e-16 relative; gradient/Hessian checks were below
4.05e-10 scaled error. In 300 synthetic driven Gonzalez steps, maximum energy
balance defect was 3.47e-16 J. These are not execution of the Rust implementation,
a full felt/BEM render, measurements or audio-deadline qualification.
