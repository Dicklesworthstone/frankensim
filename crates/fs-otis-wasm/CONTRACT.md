# fs-otis-wasm contract

This narrow browser boundary exposes the source-bounded US 31,128 topology owned by `fs-mbd::otis`.

- Inputs are normalized display coordinates and discrete source states.
- Accepted output reports generic joint axes, belt/shipper/brake state, rope-G safety release, hook-lock predicates, lower-stop state, and opposite counterpoise travel.
- Invalid coordinates or commands return a typed refusal envelope.
- The crate does not claim historical mass, speed, force, spring rate, stopping distance, engagement time, power, or unprinted dimensions.
