# Wright Flyer 3D assets

## Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum scan

- File: `smithsonian-nasm-1903-flyer.cc0.stl`
- Object: 1903 Wright Flyer (NASM A19610048000)
- Source: Smithsonian 3D Digitization / Sketchfab, mirrored on Wikimedia Commons
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)**
- Credit: The Estate of Orville Wright; Smithsonian Institution
- URL: https://3d.si.edu/object/3d/1903-wright-flyer:d8c62e5e-4ebc-11ea-b77f-2e728ce88125

The scan is a single static mesh of the restored museum artifact. It is shown as an optional overlay. Interactive warping, canard, and rudder use the parametric reconstruction in `wrightFlyerAirframe.ts`, which follows published 1903 dimensions (span 40 ft 4 in, chord 6 ft 6 in, gap ~6 ft, camber 1/20).
