Offline reference Real recording

Every instrument family: listen, compare, say what you hear.

Each card sets a render beside the real recording of the same note from the University of Iowa's instrument library: a spectrogram and a wavelet scalogram of each and of their difference, the strongest partials followed through the note, and a list of measures set against how much real recordings differ from one another. It also lists the physics the render runs and the physics it lacks. A family with no render says so. A badge only reports where the measured gaps fall; none of them is a claim that the sound is real.

This is a private review build with only this section. Your notes stay in this browser; “Copy all my notes” puts them on the clipboard so you can send them back.

Built from FrankenSim at commit %VITE_REVIEW_SHA%. Every number on this page is computed from the renders and their grades by tools/build_instruments.py; none was typed in.

Audio is level-matched per pair by the grader, and no clip was equalized or reverberated. Nobody has formally adjudicated these sounds against real instruments, and no realism claim is made beyond what each card states.
