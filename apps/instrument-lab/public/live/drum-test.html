FrankenSim live drum: reduced real-time model

Click the head to strike it. Every sound is FrankenSim's own linear drum mechanics running in WebAssembly: finite-element heads, eigenmodes, a sealed-air spring coupling both heads, a Hertz stick contact, and Rayleigh half-space radiation to a microphone. No samples, no normalization. This page is the engine's standalone test, not the site.
